Our process

We began by marking up a textbook in Russian using XML, taking note of key proper nouns (names, places..etc). This mark-up was completed using a schema we created specifically for this project. This schema can be found in our GitHub page.

In addition to marking up key nouns, we additionally marked up propogandistic terms.

Our corpus

We're using the textbook Кратктй Курс Истории СССР (A Short Lesson on the History of the USSR): a textbook printed in 1955 for third and fourth grade Soviet history classes. You can read through a digital version on the "Corpus" page of our website or go this site for the original.

We chose this text for it's ease of readability, and condensation of the information deemed necessary for the education of children in the USSR.

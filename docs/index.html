Analyzing and Presenting Soviet History

Using a primary school textbook as both a primary and secondary source

Overview:

A Project for Digital Methods in Humanistic research!

Project Members:

Arina Isayev, Olga Tseytlin, Scott Whalen, Lauren Hinrichsen.

Our Corpus:

We're using the textbook Кратктй Курс Истории СССР (A Short Lesson on the History of the USSR): a textbook printed in 1955 for third and fourth grade Soviet history classes.

Reseach Question:

How do we format and annotate textbooks on Soviet History to make them accessible to those unfamiliar with Russian culture and language? Which time periods in Russian history are more heavily propogandized, as noted by the frequency and type of propogandized terms in a Soviet-era textbook?
